Common questions

Quick answers before you call.

What should I do first if someone has been arrested?

Call as soon as you can and keep the FIR number, police station name and any papers handy. Bail is often the first step, and early advice helps.

Can I apply for anticipatory bail before an arrest?

Yes, if you fear arrest in a non-bailable offence. The application goes to the Sessions Court or the High Court, depending on the case.

My cheque bounced. What now?

You generally need to send a legal notice within 30 days of the bank's return memo. Contact me early so the timelines are not missed.

Do you take matrimonial cases by mutual consent?

Yes. Divorce by mutual consent, maintenance, custody and contested cases are all part of the practice.

Can you appear in courts outside Delhi?

Yes. Besides Delhi, I appear before the Allahabad and Rajasthan High Courts and the courts in Gurgaon.
